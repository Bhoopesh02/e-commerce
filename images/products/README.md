## How To Use

1. **Adding / Replacing Product Photos:**
   - Drop your photos (`.webp`, `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.avif`) directly into the corresponding product folder.
   - You can add multiple angles, detail shots, or color variants.

2. **Adding a New Product:**
   - Create a new folder (e.g., `cashmere-scarf` or `oxford-leather-shoes`).
   - Place your product photos inside that folder.

3. **Tell Antigravity:**
   - Tell me: *"I added new photos for [product name], please update it"* or *"Sync images for the new product"*.
   - I will automatically optimize them, place them in `frontend/public/images/products/`, and update the store's data (`products.json`).
